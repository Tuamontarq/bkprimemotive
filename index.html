BLS / ALS

Mounting structure rated to the 10G equipment-anchorage safety standard for medical conversions. The downloadable spec sheet covers the full factory specification — Maxus eDeliver 9 Ambulance L3H2, 100 kWh.

BLS / ALS Conversions

Two conversion levels on the same platform, built to public-health medical vehicle standards.

BLS

Basic Life Support

Clearly zoned patient cabin with easy-clean ABS walls, onboard oxygen system, and a complete set of first-response equipment.

ALS

Advanced Life Support

Adds a powered stretcher, ventilator, advanced defibrillator/monitor, and a negative-pressure ventilation system for infectious-case transport.
